The Australian cricket team is scheduled to tour India between January and March 2027 to play the India cricket team. The tour will consist of five Test matches, which will form part of the 2025–2027 ICC World Test Championship. In March 2026, the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) confirmed the fixtures for the tour, as a part of the 2026–27 home international season.
It is Australia's first Test tour since 2022–23.

== Venues ==
The venues for the Test series were announced in March 2026 following the BCCI's release of India's home schedule for the 2026/27 season. The venues were noted for being non-traditional Test venues for the Indian team. Chennai's M. A. Chidambaram Stadium had not hosted a Test since 2024, and Australia had not played a Test at the venue since 2013. Guwahati's Assam Cricket Association Stadium had only hosted one Test match since its opening in 2012, and JSCA International Stadium Complex in Ranchi had hosted a total of three Tests sine its first in 2017.
