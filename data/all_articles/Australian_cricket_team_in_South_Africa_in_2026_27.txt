The Australia national cricket team toured South Africa in September and October 2026 to play three One Day International (ODI) and three Test matches. The Test series formed part of the 2025–2027 World Test Championship cycle. In February 2026, Cricket South Africa (CSA) confirmed the fixtures for the tour, as a part of the 2026 home international season.
The 2026 Test series marked the first time since Australia's controversial 2017–18 tour that Australia had played South Africa in South Africa in Test cricket. South Africa won both the ODI series and Test series by 2–1. By winning the Test series, South Africa became the number 1 ranked team in Tests after nearly 10 years.

== Squads ==

On 12 September, Ottneil Baartman was ruled out from the ODI series due to a hamstring injury and was replaced by Duan Jansen. On 18 September, Lungi Ngidi was ruled out from the ODI series due to a hamstring injury and was replaced by Nqobani Mokoena. On 23 September, Kwena Maphaka was ruled out from the ODI series due to a right hamstring strain and was replaced by Lizaad Williams.
On 21 September, Billy Stanlake and Spencer Johnson were ruled out of the ODI series following a side strain and management, Jack Edwards was named in their place. On 29 September, both Nathan Ellis and Joel Davies were ruled out of the last ODI due to side strain and a hamstring strain, respectively with no replacements named. On 1 October, Josh Inglis was ruled out of the Test series due to fractured finger and was replaced by Sam Harper. Cameron Green's availability was uncertain after he sustained lower abdominal strain, Nic Maddinson was added in the squad for his cover. Later, Inglis rejoined the squad for remaining matches. 
On 15 October, Matthew Breetzke was added to South Africa's squad as a replacement for Tristan Stubbs who was dropped from the squad.

== Venues ==
In February 2026, Cricket South Africa (CSA) announced their men's team home schedule, confirming the ODI and Test venues for the series against Australia. Durban's Kingsmead Cricket Ground, Johannesburg's Wanderers Stadium, Potchefstroom's JB Marks Oval, Gqeberha's St George's Park Cricket Ground, and Cape Town's Newlands Cricket Ground were revealed to host the three ODIs and three Test matches, respectively.
