The Bangladesh cricket team are scheduled to South Africa in November and December 2026 to play two Test matches, three One Day Internationals (ODI) and three Twenty20 International (T20I). The Test series will form part of the 2025–27 WTC cycle. In February 2026, the Cricket South Africa (CSA) confirmed the fixtures for the tour, as a part of the 2026 home international season.
