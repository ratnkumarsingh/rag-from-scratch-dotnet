The 2027 ICC Men's Cricket World Cup Qualifier will be the thirteenth edition of the Cricket World Cup Qualifier, which will take place from 26 February to 21 March 2027 in Namibia and South Africa. The tournament will serve as the final stage of the qualification process for the 2027 Cricket World Cup. 
In a new process this year, the winner of the qualifier enters the second round of the World Cup, while the other three teams in the qualifier enter the first round. All the matches will be played with One Day International status.

== Qualification ==
The tournament will feature ten teams: the two teams ranked 9th and 10th (excluding the hosts, South Africa and Zimbabwe) in the ICC Men's ODI Team Rankings who failed to qualify directly along with the top four teams from the 2024–2026 Cricket World Cup League 2, and the top four teams from the 2026 Cricket World Cup Qualifier Play-off.
