The India cricket team is touring New Zealand from October to December 2026 to play two Tests, five One Day Internationals (ODIs) and five Twenty20 International (T20I) matches. The Test series forms a part of the 2025–2027 World Test Championship cycle. In June 2026, New Zealand Cricket (NZC) confirmed the fixtures for the tour, as a part of the 2026 home international season. This is the biggest bilateral series played between both nations.
India won the five match T20I series by 3–2, after winning the initial three T20Is, despite a late comeback by New Zealand.

== Squads ==

On 9 October, Mark Chapman and Zak Foulkes were ruled out of the T20I series due to hamstring and hip flexor injuries respectively. They were replaced by Josh Clarkson and Blair Tickner. On 28 October, Lockie Ferguson and Matt Henry were ruled out from the 4th and 5th T20Is due to calf injury and quad injury respectively. Later, Matthew Fisher was announced as the replacement. On 30 October, Prasidh Krishna was ruled out of the ODI series due to hamstring injury. On 31 October, Auqib Nabi who was originally in squad for only 4th and 5th ODIs was added for all five ODI matches. On 1 November, Mark Chapman and Rachin Ravindra were ruled out of the ODI series due to hamstring and shoulder injuries respectively. Later, Tim Seifert was made available for all five ODI matches while Dean Foxcroft and Henry Nicholls were added to the squad as replacements. On 6 November, Devon Conway was ruled out of the ODI series due to concussion injury. Later, Nick Kelly was included for all five ODI games. On 9 November, Lockie Ferguson was ruled out of remaining ODI matches, Matt Henry was cleared for 4th ODI and Blair Tickner was ruled out due to concussion. He was later replaced by Kristian Clarke.

== India A cricket team in New Zealand in 2026–27 ==

India A cricket team are touring New Zealand to play against New Zealand A cricket team in October and November 2026. The tour consists of three First-class matches.

=== Squad ===

On 12 October 2026, Rishabh Pant was ruled out for the first two matches and was later replaced by Kumar Kushagra. On 23 October 2026, Shikhar Mohan was added to the India A squad for the last two matches in the place of KL Rahul. Mohammed Siraj was added into the squad for the second match while Prasidh Krishna and Nitish Kumar Reddy were added to the squad for the third match. On 5 November, Rishabh Pant was ruled out of the entire series and later Jasprit Bumrah and Ravindra Jadeja were added to the squad for the third match. Tilak Varma was also added to the squad for the third match.
